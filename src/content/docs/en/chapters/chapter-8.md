TC2 is the most important reward here — it changes guff scaling to give peak efficiency up to 24 hours of a rebirth (from 30 minutes), buffing guffs by roughly 7x. The other challenges serve as an excellent EXP farm. Turn on your EXP digger before nuking bosses, and kill Boss 30 manually so you don't waste the EXP rewards.

## Post-CBlock

- Spend all EXP on Energy only for now. Energy helps the most with boss climbing, and you have Sadistic NGUs to run.
- Raise your E/M to 50M/16.66M power each to compensate for your previous RadHD and to run Sadistic NGUs.
- Resume 24 hour rebirths while climbing bosses and rebuilding your banks.
  - Before banks are full (10M+), run Sadistic NGUs in the last hour of your rebirth (same as Evil). Focus Respawn, Ygg, and EXP.
  - Once banks are full, take Looty out of daycare right after it levels up. By now it should be GDL (Grand Demon Lootzifer).
  - Pop alpha pots at the end of your rebirth to run Sad NGUs to softcap. Optimize your gear to full NGU — no respawn or anything else.
  - Once you've reached early softcaps (up to Adv A and Number), run sNGU either daily at the end of your rebirth (if you have a wearable GDL) or do a weekly sNGU day (take GDL out and run full NGU gear for a day).
- Hold off on your first Hackday until your planned HD gain would exceed 1.3x adv. The rapid growth of guffins means you don't want to waste AP. Stick to passive hacking (PP, QP, EXP) until T10 LRB.

The meta path alternates between muff rebirths and LRBs:
- Muff RBs from entry CBlock to West World snipe
- LRB from WW snipe to T10v1
- Muff RBs from T10v1 to T10v2
- LRB from T10v2 to T11v1
- Muff RBs from T11v1 to T11v2
- LRB from T11v2 to T12v1
- Muff RBs from T12v1 to T12v2 (suggested 4.5k%+ adv guff before final LRB)
- LRB from T12v2 to THE END

### Muff Usage

- Aim to get 2 uses of the muff per rebirth with slightly shorter than 24 hour rebirths.
  - Beast's Fertilizer and Five O'Clock Shadow reduce fruit/beard timers.
  - With max fertilizer, all T24 fruits finish growing at 22:48 into the rebirth.
- Before a rebirth, use a Muff, RB (1 proc), follow your normal RB for 23 hours, then RB (2nd proc).
- Daily AP income of 26-28k AP/day affords a muff every 2 days sustainably.

## Early Sadistic CBlock

Following the same logic as the early Evil CBlock, you can do a challenge block between T10v1 and T10v2. The gap between T10v1 and T10v2 is a roughly 3 week wall, and doing a CB here saves you from having to do it one and a half months later when you'd rather be pushing other content.

Challenge list:
- Troll Challenge 5 (TC7 if you scum trolls) — MUST
- All No Rebirth challenges (if you skipped any in the entry CBlock)
- All Evil 24HR challenges (do 8 if you can't stomach all)
- All Sad Blind challenges
- 4 Sad 24HR challenges

If you don't scum trolls, TC5 gives 10% card speed for a month and a half. If you do scum trolls, TC7 gives 10% card speed plus mayo speed and an accessory slot (another 5% wish speed). More daycare speed is also welcome, especially before Lootz/A9. Doing them early gives you more AP, more guffin slots earlier, and lets you stack Sad guff bonuses for longer.

Reference stats: around 500k AWG and 50k P. Your hacks will likely be lower than normal, and you'll probably have almost zero Number-related guffin bonuses. TC7 can be completed in around 1:45 with 2x 30 minute scums (fruit activation). Keep magic fill time low, even at the cost of removing magic cap gear.

If you skip some challenges, you can finish them post-T11v1. If you do all of them, you can do the remaining Sad 24HR challenges (up to 7) for the AP — you'll need every last drop for infusers.

## MacGuffins, Acc Slots, and Muffins

If you're F2P and playing daily, you probably only have 2-3 AP guffin slots and the final accessory slot left. After Sad TC2, muffining becomes immensely powerful. Here are two approaches:

### Speedrun Pace

If you're aiming for a fast Sadistic clear, muffin as soon as possible — even with only 2-3 AP slots. At this point you probably have 13 guffin slots (10 main, 4 Number, rest lower priority). The Sadistic guff priority is:

Adv > R3 Pow > R3 Cap > EPow > MPow > ECap > MCap > eNGU > mNGU > R3 Bar > Blood > Aug > Number > Stat > DC > Gold > M Wandoos > E Wandoos > Bars

DC is low because it's cube-rooted since Choco. Boss climbing is the real issue in Sadistic, not drop chance.

With 13 slots you can reach up to Augment (or Number if you skip R3 Bar). Buy at least 2 more guffin slots to cover the 14 essential guffins, using the last slot to cycle between the remainder. Start muffining and buy slots later when you can save up. The final accessory slot is useful (5% wish speed at best), but guffins are far more impactful. Save for the acc slot after 15 guffin slots.

### Chill Pace

If you prefer a slower pace, buy up to 18 guffin slots and the final acc slot first, then muffin later. This is much slower, but you won't need to worry about pushing bosses as aggressively.

Don't worry about EM bar guffins — your fill time won't be too bad without them. You can always buy more base bars since they're cheaper than power and cap. Wandoos guffins are essentially useless; you'll never need Wandoos XL until the very end.

## T10 LRB

Your first Sadistic LRB aims to take you from West World snipe to T10v1. Start the LRB at WW snipe stats (2.65e27/830e24) and begin with a Hackday.

- Spend all EXP on Adventure Power during the LRB.
- Focus PP/QP on Adventure Perks/Quirks.
- Extend the LRB to max WW gear and grab a single secret drop for daycare.
- Time your sNGU day with delta pots (if you have them) for a massive adv stat boost mid-LRB.
- Cast only Adv cards with MEH+ rarity to speed up the LRB.
- For QP and PP, only buy Adventure stats during the LRB. Never buy them outside an LRB unless they're extremely cheap.

The LRB boils down to two activities: maxing BTS/WW gear and boosting them to perform a Hackday, and running sNGU with delta pots. Both take about 2 days. End the LRB as soon as possible — the longer it is, the more guffin gain you lose. Aim for around 5 days max.

After killing T10v1, max WW set and grab the secret drop (level 1 is fine).

## T10v2 Wall

The T10v2 wall is 2-3 weeks with guff RBs. There's a Hackday mid-wall — double your E/M to 200M/66M power after each HD. During this time you can also do the [Early Sadistic CBlock](#early-sadistic-cblock) to speed things up.

Once you can snipe Breadverse/70s, start running sNGU with NGU gear. Start saving for the NGU quirk post-T10v2.

Snipe Bread/70s mostly for gold — the sets aren't worth using until you can idle them with Beast Mode.

## Daycare, Lootz, and A9P

Once you obtain the final tier of Looty (Grand Demon Lootzifer) and Pendant (A9P), wear them immediately and level them via shockwaves — same for earlier tiers. You can get 2-3 shockwaves per day and should max them within 1-2 months. The only exception is if you plan to keep playing after the game ends — keep a spare Lootz/A9P unmaxed.

## Cards, Mayos, Tags, and Casting

Start casting PP and QP cards after reaching Tier 4. Your main focus throughout the game should be leveling the card trinity: Adventure, PP, and QP. Everything else can be skimped on.

Tag the trinity first, and after obtaining the wish tag slot, tag Wish cards. Post-T10, you're recommended to start tagging EMngu cards, but it's better to hold off. Your wish tier is probably around Tier 6 (4 tiers left to max), while EMngu has 9 more tiers. Since they scale similarly, you benefit more from tagging Wish cards, which indirectly boost every other card. You don't run sNGU full-time yet anyway. Start thinking about tagging EMngu cards post-T10v2 or near T11 LRB.

Start yeeting meh cards if mayo is a concern. If you have extra AP, use infusers like muffs — infusers affect mayo fruit rewards.

## T11 LRB

The question is when to start. Conventional advice recommends 2k% adv guff, but 2k% is still early — your average muff rebirth gives about 2% adventure bonus daily, so there's plenty left to milk.

Start the T11 LRB around when you can snipe Breadverse/70s in a 24 hour rebirth (roughly 70s snipe stats). Starting too early hurts your guffin gains since the LRB prevents muff RBs. Time the LRB with your next Hackday — plan the HD with max 70s gear and weenie accessories. This saves 40k AP since the R3 beta pot gets used inside the LRB.

Run sNGU full-time inside the LRB. With pots and bread/70s gear, you can BB 5/2 sNGUs. Optimize for NGUs to run them fully throughout the LRB — wishes can take a hit since you want to end the LRB as soon as possible. Cast only Adv cards with MEH+ rarity, plus some DC cards to help max 70s and weenies. For QP and PP, buy only Adventure stats until the LRB ends — you can finally buy the Adventure milestone here (don't buy it before the LRB).

After killing T11v1, max the weenie set and end the LRB. Don't bother with Halloweenies until post-T11.

## Chonkers

Buying the BIG CHONKER CARDS Quirk unlocks Chonker drops — cards with guaranteed max rarity (1.2x) that cost 21-29 mayo. Chonkers have a separate timer than regular cards.
- Cast any Chonkers matching the tags/bonuses you'd normally cast.
- If you don't have Card Recycling: Card Spawn perk, keep unwanted Chonkers until you've bought it — yeeting them advances the Chonker timer by 25%.

## E/M Hardcaps

- E/M Cap is hardcapped at 9e18 (9Q). Stop buying at 3.5e12 (3.5T) cap.
  - 3.5T cap reaches hardcap with duct tape, all cap wishes, perks 1-4, quirks 1-2, and 2500 guff.
  - You can stop buying E/M Bars whenever you reach a desired cap time.
- E/M Power is hardcapped at 1e18 (1Q) pre-pots. Stop buying at 4e8 (400M) power.
- For R3, you can stop around 50k-60k power (cap in ratio).
  - R3 hardcaps are the same as E/M, but 50k-60k is enough to finish all hacks and wishes.

Note: This is NOT an exhaustive list of good items or the only items to keep. These are just items obtainable in Sadistic worth acknowledging. Most E/M/R3/Wish items are not included — use GO to track items that fit your loadouts. Generally, any item with a unique special bonus (AT / wandoos / respawn / cooldown / NGUs) is worth keeping.

- Keep Forever:
  - Red Shirt (respawn)
  - Carbon Rod (ygg yield)
