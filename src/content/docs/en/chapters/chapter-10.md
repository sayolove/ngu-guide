THE END IS NEAR. This chapter is short and straightforward — push through the final titan versions, prepare for the final LRB, and kill THE TRAITOR.

## Post-T12: Pushing to THE END

After the T12 LRB, get every card tier you need, especially Stat A/D. Max Stat AD, then cast a pen to reach Tier 19. Cast only 1 AD card and never cast another AD card again.

Once you reach 2.5k% EM cap guff, you can run 2 additional guffins — swap them for EM Wandoos or Gold. Run sNGU up through NGU NGU and keep Adventure B at half BB at all times. NGU PP isn't worth running 24/7.

Now all you need to do is rebirth until you have 50k R3 power, then it's time for the final LRB.

Continue guff RBs until around 4.5k% adventure guff, then start the final LRB. You can start sooner if desired — it will just take longer.

### Tips for THE END

- If your QP gain is slow, wear Quest Drops gear (Choffice Hat + Netherlands).
- Once you can BB all blood rituals, blood gain is mostly affected by blood guff %.
- Run Wandoos XL in the final LRB to reach Boss 300.
  - If you're unable to reach 300, consider rebirthing for the Number gain.
  - You can also use pens on A/D chonkers to take advantage of tier scaling.
- At a certain point, Health Regen becomes more useful than Power/Toughness for the T14 fight.
  - If you reach 150 Decillion / 150e33 Power, stop buying Power and switch to HP Regen.

## Final LRB

Say goodbye to the Rebirth button. Start the final LRB when you can finish all hacks in 2 Hackdays and reach Boss 300.

You can split the Hackday into 2 separate ones:
1. First Hackday: Only the Number-related hacks (A/D, Aug, Blood, and Number). Perform 1-2 rebirths after. You only need to finish these in the first HD to maximize your boss climbing power.
2. Second Hackday: The remaining hacks.

Within a 30 day rebirth, you can get up to 8 free bosses from Basic Training, Wandoos, Fu, and FoPa. Your Augment buster count (check GO) plus buying jerks/EXP can give an additional 2-3 bosses. That's at least 10 bosses minimum. If you start the LRB at 1e109 Number without finishing the Number hacks and your previous RB's highest boss was 288, you should reach Boss 300 within 15 days (run augment for days — aim for 1.6M/3.2M busters).

Once you can kill T12v2, max the accessories and start your final Hackday. Finish the remaining hacks with 50k R3 power, 500% card hack, all R3 P/C upgrades except the final one, and 3.2k/1.8k R3 P/C guff.

After reaching Boss 300:
- Do only Adventure wishes — ignore every other wish to maximize the speed of the final Adventure wish (1 slot).
- If the completion time for the final Adventure wish exceeds the remaining time in the 30 day rebirth, don't run NGU PP.
- Once you've reached the recommended card bonus from the general card limits, cast only Adv cards regardless of rarity. You can cast Good+ PP/QP cards if you haven't bought the final END piece, but don't prioritize them — Adventure is always the bottleneck.
- All your AP should be spent on infusers.
- If you've bought the final PP piece, go farm boosts in Netherland until you have enough Adventure stats to kill THE TRAITOR.
- Optimize for full Respawn and Quest Drop with some NGU to keep Adventure B BB'd (if you've maxed the final Adventure wish).
- After killing THE TRAITOR, cast only QP cards and do manual minoring to grab the final QP END piece if it isn't finished yet.

## Wishes

The general rule for wishes: always run the "big" wishes — Tag Slot, Acc Slot, and card trinity tier ups (Adv, QP, PP) — when they are 1 week or less with one or two wish slots. Everything else comes after those. Fast Card 5 and Chonker-related wishes aren't worth getting.
