Welcome to the longest slog of Sadistic. After the T11 LRB you'll have a maxed weenie set and need to push through the remaining T11 content.

## Post-T11: Reaching 70s

You might struggle a bit reaching Boss 224 in 1 hour to activate T11v3. The solution is to cast A/D cards if you haven't already — buy some cheap A/D tier upgrades and cast just 1 card. You can also switch to MEH while equipping 69 gear if full Beast Mode is too expensive.

At this point, if you've bought the final accessory slot and have 15 guffin slots, the rest of your AP should go toward infusers.

For sNGU, you should be able to permanently BB 5/2 with 400M/133M EM power post-LRB. For reference, 500/200 EMngu card % should be enough to BB 5/2 — though Adventure A might be a little expensive.

After maxing T11v1 set, you can do the final CBlock if you haven't, or do 2-3 more Sad 24HR challenges for AP.

## Cards, Mayos, Tags, and Casting

Same approach as the previous chapter, with one change: post-T11 LRB, start casting and tagging EMngu cards to maintain 5/2 BB without potions. Once you can BB up to Power A and Number, shift back to tagging and casting Wish cards.

If your Wish tier is approaching 500%, switch to Hack or MNGu cards — you'll need them later. Avoid casting any card types not mentioned above — they don't meaningfully help progression.

### Card Limits

You can stop casting these card types after they reach these % bonus values:
- Hack/Wish: 500%
- Drop Chance: 1000%
- NGU's: 2500% (each)
- A/D: 100m%

## T11v2 Wall and T12 LRB

Just like the previous chapter, there's a Hackday midway through the wall. Get your Magic to 400M power and never look at E/M in the EXP shop again. T11v2 stats typically come around 3400% adventure guff, roughly a month after beating T11v1.

Once you kill T11v2, it's time for the T12 LRB. Your adventure guff should be around 3.5k%. Unlike the T11 LRB, there's no benefit to delaying here — your daily guffin gains are much smaller compared to your total gain, and T12 provides endgame gear that lets you BB sNGU full-time, which is critical since sNGU is time-gated.

Time the LRB with your next Hackday. Plan the HD with duck gear, some Nether accessories, and T11v4 accessories. Run Adventure B the entire LRB. Cast only Adv cards with MEH+ rarity and some DC cards. Same rule for PP and QP — buy only Adventure stats until the LRB ends.

This LRB will be long — expect at least 2 weeks. LRB segments broken down:
- About a week each to reach T11v3 and T11v4
- Then roughly 3 days to T12
- T11v4 accessories are amazing

Once you can kill T12v1, max Nether gear and end the LRB.

### Zones

**Construction gear** will be quickly outclassed by Duck and Nether, but is useful for now. Still not worth sniping — wait until idle.

**Duck and Nether** are the real reasons for the T12 LRB. Snipe them as soon as possible:
- Gear will be used through the end of the game
- T12 gear itself is mediocre, but the accessories are good. The weapon is useful for sniping until Nether.
- Extend the LRB to max Nether after T12.

Focus Adventure stats (Adv Tier, Perk 4-5, Quirk 4-5) and R3 for a final Hackday.
